Pre-flight checklist

Items that cause most DNS migration incidents — verify each before switching nameservers.
  • DNSSEC: confirm the old provider is unsigned and no stale DS records exist.
  • CAA: confirm the target provider's certificate authorities are allowed.
  • Nameservers: verify the target provider's assigned pair for each zone.
  • Mail: MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records exist and match the current provider.
  • Validation records: do not delete _dnsauth, trust-provider or similar records without checking certificate dependencies.
  • Zone clock: free Cloudflare zones pending for more than 28 days are deleted — switch in time.
  • Rollback: keep the old provider's records until the observation period ends.
